## Why this matters for TC-8020
- TC-8020 needs remediation in stream 2.2.x (2.2.0, 2.2.1 and 2.2.2 ship quinn-proto 0.11.9 or 0.11.12, below 0.11.14). The fix is a Cargo dependency bump plus downstream propagation.
- TC-8019 is a different CVE on the same crate. If engineer-b's triage creates a quinn-proto bump task for 2.2.x that targets **>= 0.11.14**, that task would also cover CVE-2026-31812. Creating our own tasks now would duplicate theirs.
- The two triages could also reach Step 7.5.3 (release-Task dedup) at the same time, before either has linked a remediation Task to the release Task. Dedup would then fail to catch the overlap. Step 7 exists to stop this race.
