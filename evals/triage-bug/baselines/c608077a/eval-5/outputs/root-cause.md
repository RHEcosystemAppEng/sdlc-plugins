## 1. What is broken

The Dark Mode preference set in Settings > Appearance does not survive a full browser restart. On reload, the app falls back to the default light theme, and the toggle shows OFF.

## 2. Why it is broken (hypothesis, ranked)

The symptom is "works within a session, lost after the browser is closed". That points to a storage scope or load-order problem, not a rendering problem:

1. **Session-scoped or in-memory storage (most likely).** The toggle writes the preference to component or app state, `sessionStorage`, or a session cookie (no `Expires`/`Max-Age`). All three are cleared when the browser closes.
2. **Write without read-back.** The preference is saved durably (to `localStorage` or a backend user-preferences endpoint), but startup never reads it. The theme provider initializes to the hard-coded default (`light`) and the toggle is bound to that default.
3. **Backend preference not persisted.** If preferences are stored server-side in acme-backend, the update handler may not commit the value, or the GET response may leave out the theme field, so the client gets the default.
