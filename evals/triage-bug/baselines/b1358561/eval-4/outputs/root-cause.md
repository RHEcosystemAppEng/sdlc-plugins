## What is broken

`GET /api/v2/advisories` leaves out the `X-Total-Count` and `Link` (`rel="next"`) pagination headers whenever the date-range filters `publishedAfter` and/or `publishedBefore` are present. The response body (filtered and limited results) is correct.

## Why it is broken

When date-range filters are present, the advisories list path does not produce pagination metadata, or does not pass it to the header-writing step. The unfiltered path emits the headers correctly. So the pagination/header mechanism itself works. The date-filtered path bypasses it, either through a separate branch returning a bare list, or through a total-count query that is not built or fails silently when date predicates are applied.
